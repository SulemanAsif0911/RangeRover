WHERE IT BEGAN

RANGE ROVER
CLASSIC

Conceived for farmers and estate owners who needed a vehicle equally at home fording a river or arriving at the county show, the original Range Rover accidentally invented an entire category. Coil springs and permanent four‑wheel drive gave it the manners of a saloon car everywhere else it went.
