WHERE IT BEGAN

RANGE ROVER
CLASSIC

Conceived for farmers and estate owners who needed a vehicle equally at home fording a river or arriving at the county show, the original Range Rover accidentally invented an entire category. Coil springs and permanent four‑wheel drive gave it the manners of a saloon car everywhere else it went.

BORN TO CORNER

RANGE ROVER
SPORT

Lower, stiffer, and considerably more eager to be hurried, the Sport gave the range a performance‑first sibling — the presence of a Range Rover with the reflexes of something two classes smaller. A supercharged V8 turned "sport" from a suffix into a promise.
